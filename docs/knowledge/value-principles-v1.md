# Project value research principles

Version 1.0 — 2026-10-16. Original project notes; no book excerpts.
Internal research use: storage, indexing and model context are permitted for this project.
These are hypotheses and review requirements, not validated investment rules.

## Cash flows and assumptions

Explain the source and units of every cash-flow input. Separate historical observations
from forecasts. Compare several plausible assumptions instead of treating a single
valuation estimate as a known price target. Record what would invalidate the thesis.

## Accounting and downside

Review leverage, cash conversion and accounting changes alongside profitability.
Identify missing disclosures and uncertainty. A low valuation ratio alone does not
establish that an asset is undervalued. ETF holdings require a different interpretation
from an operating company's financial statements.

## Time boundary

Use the public availability of the actual filing version, not its accounting period,
to determine whether an observation could have been known at a decision time.
These notes were written in 2026 and are not evidence of a historical strategy.
