# Project momentum research principles

Version 1.0 — 2026-10-16. Original project notes; no book excerpts.
Internal research use: storage, indexing and model context are permitted for this project.
These are hypotheses and review requirements, not validated investment rules.

## Signal specification

Define the observation window, rebalance schedule, ranking universe and signal formula
before evaluating outcomes. Separate cross-sectional relative strength from time-series
trend. An example in a different market or asset class does not establish a US equity edge.

## Costs and reversals

Evaluate turnover, delayed fills, gaps, spread and concentration. Test unfavorable market
regimes and reversals. A favorable in-sample result is a reason for further validation,
not permission to trade or tune parameters on the final evaluation period.

## Decision boundary

Prices used to form a signal must precede the decision. The execution price belongs to
the later execution stage and must not be visible to an analyst producing that signal.
