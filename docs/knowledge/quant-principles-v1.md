# Project quantitative research principles

Version 1.0 — 2026-10-16. Original project notes; no book excerpts.
Internal research use: storage, indexing and model context are permitted for this project.
These are hypotheses and review requirements, not validated investment rules.

## Experiment specification

Record hypotheses, data versions, transformations, evaluation periods and all parameter
searches before interpreting results. Preserve failed experiments. Compare with simple
baselines under the same universe, information boundary and transaction-cost assumptions.

## Dependence and evidence

Value and momentum can both be quantified; a quantitative role does not create an
independent third vote. Check overlapping evidence and exposures. Separate a numeric
calculation, an empirical finding and an analyst's interpretation in every explanation.

## Limits and abstention

Report data omissions, survivorship, revisions and model knowledge contamination.
Missing or contradictory evidence should trigger a recorded abstention or review.
Source text, retrieved documents and model proposals cannot change risk or order authority.
